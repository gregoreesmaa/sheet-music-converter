- **Never Guess Synthetic Labels with a Detector:** Training system/measure supervision must come deterministically from the engraver/renderer (`big500-symbox`, `system-breaks`, reprojection), never from classical barline heuristics.
- **No Static Unrectified 1D Column Collapse:** Do NOT replace `MvdNet.proj` with a static `Conv2d(768, d, (32, 1))` on unrectified strips (all 5 expert subagents proved this destroys multi-staff polyphonic pitch selection). Preserve 2D vertical resolution `(H/8 = 32)` on unrectified strips, or apply vertical pooling only after Canonical Multi-Staff Slot Rectification (`T2.1`).
- **Metric & Domain Hygiene:** Always report `clean` and `degraded` accuracy/gaps **separately** (never pool `clean + degraded` 50/50 when evaluating a single-domain or mixed checkpoint). Use true standardized white paper (`train.WHITE`) and `swap_other_strip` baselines for grounding probes.
- **Licenses Stay Permissive (MIT/BSD/Apache):** No AGPL/GPL dependencies in the inference or training path.
